Hosted payment

A simple manual process.

You pay through Buy Me a Coffee or another method listed in your Billing page. Darith does not collect card details or online banking credentials.
  1. Pay outside Darith. Use Buy Me a Coffee, bank transfer, PayPal, Revolut, Wise, or the payment method currently listed in Billing.
  2. Use your reference. Include the personal Darith payment reference when the payment service allows it.
  3. Report the payment. Select I have paid in Billing. An administrator verifies it and updates your access date.

Buy Me a Coffee can also create a recurring monthly contribution. Hosted access is still matched and verified manually by the Darith administrator.

Clear answers

Before you choose.

Does the free version have fewer budgeting features?

No. The self-hosted repository provides the same core personal finance workflow. The difference is who operates and maintains the server.

Will Darith ask for my card or bank login?

No. Darith does not connect to your bank and does not collect card details. Hosted payment happens through the separate method shown in Billing.

What does the monthly contribution pay for?

It supports online availability, server costs, application maintenance, updates, and backups for the hosted service.

How does the free trial work?

A new hosted account receives {{ trial_days }} days of access without entering a card. After the trial, hosted access requires a manually verified payment or administrator-granted access.
