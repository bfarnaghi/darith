01

Begin here

A useful first setup

Darith works best when your starting balances and monthly plans are correct. This setup takes only a few minutes.

  1. 1
    Choose your display

    Open Settings, choose a currency and theme, and optionally add a profile picture. Currency changes how amounts are shown; it does not convert money between currencies.

  2. 2
    Add your accounts

    Enter the current balance of each bank or cash account. Turn off Include in monthly budget for money you only want to track.

  3. 3
    Set expected daily spending

    On Overview, choose Edit beside Daily spending plan. Enter a normal daily amount for food, transport, and other everyday spending.

  4. 4
    Add monthly plans

    Add salary and other repeating income, then rent, bills, subscriptions, and other repeating expenses with their first and optional last dates.

  5. 5
    Add saving goals

    Choose a monthly amount or a target amount and date. Select the bank account that will fund each goal.

  6. 6
    Record daily activity

    Add income and expenses when they happen. Darith updates the selected account and recalculates the month immediately.

Best starting point

Use the balance your bank shows today. Do not add old transactions unless you also adjust the starting balance to match them.

02

Finding your way

Navigation and quick actions

Desktop sidebar

Move between Overview, Accounts, Transactions, Monthly plans, and Categories. Tutorial, feedback, billing, and logout are available near your profile.

Mobile navigation

Use the bottom bar for Home, Accounts, Activity, and Plans. The large plus button opens a new expense. Categories, Settings, Tutorial, export, and account actions are in the profile menu.

Top actions

Transfer moves existing money. Income adds money to an account. Expense subtracts money from an account.

Messages

After an action, a message confirms what happened or explains why it could not be completed.

03

Your monthly picture

Overview

Overview shows the difference between the money in your bank and the money that is safe to spend.

Safe to spend today
The most extra money you can spend today while keeping your future income, expenses, savings, and daily spending on track.
Extra per day
The safe-to-spend amount divided by the days left in this month, including today.
Current bank balance
The total money in bank and cash accounts that are included in your budget. Savings goals and tracking-only accounts are separate.
Balance expected at month end
The bank balance Darith expects at the end of this month after planned income, expenses, daily spending, and savings. This is not all free to spend.
Money timeline
A day-by-day view. Move the slider to see your bank balance, what is still planned, and how much is safe to spend on each day.
Money in your plan−Daily spending−Money Darith must keep aside=Safe to spend
Why Darith checks every future day

Darith checks each day because income may arrive after an expense. Money is not treated as available before it arrives. This helps Darith find days when your money could become too low, even if the whole month looks fine.

Budget status

Good

Your plan looks good and you have some extra money you can safely spend.

Tight

Your plan is still okay, but there is only a small amount of extra money.

Not enough

Darith expects your money to become too low on a future day. Check your income, expenses, savings, or daily spending.

The six detail figures

Current bank balance

Total across included accounts.

Income this month

Income already received plus repeating income still due.

Expenses this month

Expenses already paid plus repeating expenses still due.

Expected daily costs

Your daily estimate and its total for the remaining days.

Saving due

This month's goal contributions that have not yet been funded.

Saved in goals

Total money kept in active saving goals. It is separate from money you can spend.

Activity, reminders, and coming up

Recent transactions shows the latest entries. Coming up shows repeating income and expenses due later this month. When a goal contribution is ready, Fund your goals shows the amount and source account; Mark saved creates a real transfer into the goal.

The eye button hides financial values across the dashboard and remembers that choice for your user. The gear button opens Settings.

04

Where money lives

Accounts

Bank and cash accounts

Create an account with a name and its current balance. Income, expenses, and transfers update this balance automatically.

Included accounts

Use these for money available to your monthly budget. Their balances and connected plans affect Overview.

Tracking-only accounts

Turn off Include in monthly budget for money you want to record but do not want Darith to use in safe-to-spend checks.

Goal accounts

Each saving goal has its own balance in Accounts. Money in a saving goal is not counted as money you can spend.

Select the three-dot button on an account to rename it, correct its current balance, change its budget inclusion, or remove it. Removing a bank account keeps transaction history, but an account still used by a transfer or goal may need those links changed first.

05

What already happened

Transactions

Income

Add money that has already arrived. Choose its description, amount, date, category, and destination bank account.

Expense

Record money already spent. Darith subtracts it from the selected bank account.

Transfer

Move money between bank accounts and saving goals. The source must have enough money.

Export CSV

Download your Darith data in a spreadsheet-friendly file for your own records or analysis.

Past and future dates

Manual transactions and transfers represent completed activity, so they cannot be dated in the future. Use Monthly plans for future income and charges.

Editing and deleting

Select a transaction to edit or remove it. When you edit a transaction, Darith removes the old change and applies the new one. In Settings > Transactions, choose what deletion does:

  • Update balances automatically: deleting an expense restores its amount; deleting income removes its amount; deleting a transfer restores both sides.
  • Leave balances unchanged: only the activity record is removed, and you correct account balances yourself if needed.

Deleting one item from a monthly plan skips only that item. Future months stay planned.

06

What should repeat

Monthly plans

Plans are for income, expenses, and savings you know will happen later. Darith uses them before their date, then records them when they happen.

Monthly income

Add salary or other monthly income. Choose the first payment date, optional final date, category, and receiving bank account.

Monthly expenses

Add rent, bills, subscriptions, or other monthly charges. Choose the first charge date, optional final date, category, and paying bank account.

Date handling

The day in the first date becomes the monthly due day. A date such as the 31st is moved to the last valid day in shorter months.

Automatic posting

On or after the date, Darith adds each planned item once and updates the account. Darith checks due items when the dashboard opens and the server can also check them automatically.

Editing a plan changes future items. Past transactions stay the same. Deleting a plan stops future items but keeps past activity.

07

Money with a purpose

Saving goals

Create a goal in one of two ways:

Ongoing goal

Enter a fixed monthly amount, such as 400 each month. Darith reserves that amount until the goal is funded.

Dated target

Enter a target amount and target date. Darith calculates the monthly contribution needed from the amount still remaining.

Also enter anything already saved, the first monthly saving date, and a default funding account. The goal then has a separate balance shown in Accounts.

How funding works

  1. A reminder shows the contribution still due for the active month.
  2. Mark saved transfers the amount from the chosen bank account into the goal immediately.
  3. On the goal's monthly date, Darith can fund it automatically when the source account has enough money.
  4. Each goal is funded at most once for a month, and the transfer appears in Activity.
Removing a goal

Move its remaining balance back to a bank account first. Darith only removes a goal account when its balance is zero, and keeps its transfer history.

08

Keep activity understandable

Categories

Income and expense categories are separate. Darith starts each user with practical defaults, and you can add, rename, or remove your own. Categories make the activity list easier to read and are included in the CSV export.

Each user's categories are private to that user. Removing a category does not remove the related transaction; the transaction remains in Activity without that category.

09

Make Darith yours

Settings

Appearance

Profile picture

JPEG, PNG, or WebP up to 2 MB and 1200 by 1200 pixels.

Theme

Ocean, Forest, Graphite, or Purple. The choice is saved only for your user.

Currency

EUR, USD, GBP, CHF, CAD, AUD, or Iranian Toman (IRT). This is display formatting, not currency conversion.

Language

Choose English, Persian, Italian, French, Spanish, or Dutch. Darith saves the choice to your account, and Persian uses a right-to-left layout.

Status GIFs

You can add one private GIF for Good, Tight, and Not enough. Each is limited to 2 MB, 1200 by 1200 pixels, and 300 frames. A new upload replaces the previous file, and Remove GIF clears it.

Planning

Emergency buffer

Money Darith always keeps aside. The default is 0.

Months to check

Choose 1, 2, or 3 months ahead. One month is the default.

Money timeline

Choose if the day-by-day slider is shown on Overview.

Transactions

Choose if deleting a transaction changes the account balance back, or leaves the balance as it is.

Security

Auto lock

Choose Off, 1, 5, 15, or 30 minutes. Darith locks after you do not use it for that time.

Darith PIN

Create a 4 to 8 digit PIN for unlocking. The saved value is securely hashed rather than stored as readable digits.

Passkey

Add a passkey to unlock with your device's fingerprint, Face ID, screen lock, or security key. Passkeys require HTTPS and a supported browser.

Fingerprint and Face ID privacy

Your device checks your fingerprint or face. Darith only receives a yes/no result, not your fingerprint or face data.

Before turning on auto lock, add at least one way to unlock Darith. Keep a working PIN or passkey before removing your last available method.

Account

The Account tab can permanently delete your Darith user and live data. To delete your account, enter your password, tick the confirmation box, type the text shown on screen, and confirm one last time. This action cannot be undone; encrypted historical backups may remain until normal backup rotation removes them.

10

Control and trust

Privacy, data, and feedback

No bank credentials

Darith never asks for your online banking username, password, card number, or bank API access. You enter only the financial information you choose.

Private by user

Accounts, transactions, categories, goals, settings, profile images, and GIFs are scoped to the signed-in user. Other users cannot view them.

Limited admin screens

The normal admin interface manages usernames, email addresses, passwords, subscriptions, and submitted feedback without listing users' financial records.

Safe connection and backups

darith.app uses HTTPS for data in transit and supports encrypted database and media backups. The server operator still has technical access for maintenance; Darith is not end-to-end encrypted.

Use Export CSV when you want a copy of your data. Use Feedback in the dashboard to send a comment to the operator; only the message, page context, and your account identity are sent.

11

Ways to use Darith

Free locally or hosted online

Individuals can run Darith on their own computer for free using the local setup instructions on GitHub. Hosted access at darith.app includes a 45-day trial with no card required.

Hosted payments are manual. Pay through the listed method, report the payment from Billing, and an administrator verifies it and sets your access date. Darith does not collect payment card or bank credentials.
